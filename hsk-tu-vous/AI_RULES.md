# HSK AI — active production guardrails

These are the **current global policies** for every agent and chat. They are deliberately not a copy of settings, calendar entries, creative Lab procedures or technical implementation details.

## Ownership and precedence

Read `AGENTS.md` for the ownership map and migration protocol. In case of conflict, the latest explicit owner instruction authorizes a **migration of the owning source**, not a silent override in one chat. Stop and reconcile inconsistent active files before production or publication.

- `AI_RULES.md`: global behavior, editorial limits, publishing authorization and safety.
- `content/languages.json`: sole source of default production languages, locale codes and language-specific content roots.
- Each configured content root's `voice-profile.json`: sole source of that language's provider/voice/settings and approval state.
- Each configured content root's `cta-lab.json`: sole source of that language's current CTA wording/offer and correction state; `content/CTA_LAB.md` describes its use.
- Each configured content root's `hook-lab.json` and shared `content/HOOK_LAB.md`: language-specific hook experiments, corrections, readbacks and common editorial criteria.
- `content/calendar.json`: dates, IDs, schedule and timezone.
- `content/visual-system.json`: numeric design and caption settings; `content/EDITING_SPEC.md` explains editing technique.
- `content/EPISODE_SCHEMA.md`: persisted episode/review record shape.
- `content/WORKFLOW.md`: operational order, error handling and preview process.

**Rule changes are migrations.** Update the owning source, search active documents/configuration/code for all dependent copies, remove or reconcile superseded active statements, run `npm run check:consistency` and test the affected flow. Preserve historical experiment data and already approved/published snapshots as historical evidence; never relabel old content as current. No automated check can detect all semantic contradictions, so agent review remains mandatory.

## Approved voice

- The selected language's configured `voice-profile.json` is the **only place** where its active provider, Gemini models, locale, voice, spoken style, audio format and approval status are defined. Do not duplicate those settings here or infer approval from another language's profile.
- The approved Gemini **models and voice identity** stay fixed in each language's `voice-profile.json`. The current production API transport defaults to OpenRouter (`HSK_AI_VOICE_API_PROVIDER=openrouter`); direct Gemini requires an explicit `gemini-direct` setting and `HSK_AI_ALLOW_GEMINI_API=true`. Read actual runtime transport and key availability before work. `content:voice` generates audio, validates exact word timestamps and imports only matching speech. Never change voice/model/provider transport to conceal an error.
- The owner-approved profile currently stored in `content/voice-profile.json` remains the main fixed voice for the original Russian series. Reuse it automatically; never ask the owner to identify, select, or reconfirm that voice for each episode. A different voice/provider or changed settings require an explicit owner decision and a freshly approved sample. This also applies to a new language profile. A profile with `modelDecisionRequired` cannot make an API request until the owner decides on that concrete model; afterward, while `pending-owner-approval`, production is limited to its designated sample episode. Only the owner can approve the listened-to sample; never manufacture or inherit an approval record.
- Narration may mix Russian/Tajik explanation and Mandarin examples. For the **direct Gemini** transcription transport, request configured locale + Mandarin hints; the OpenRouter adapter sends only **one** ISO-639-1 hint per request (`tg`/`ru`/`zh`, verified to change its output on 2026-11-08), so mixed audio relies on per-language passes. Record the hint actually sent; never claim a multi-language hint on OpenRouter. Preserve actual verbatim word timestamps, normalized exact-token validation and source-language spelling.
- In Chinese-learning videos, speak the target Mandarin words and at least one core example phrase aloud; do not leave the Chinese content only on screen.
- Send the approved narration to TTS exactly as written, including the fixed “HSK AI” brand spelling. Keep acronym handling in the transcription alignment check; do not change CTA wording or substitute a phonetic spelling to work around a mismatch.
- The selected transport receives approved narration and generated speech for TTS/STT. Keep `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` (current default), and any explicitly enabled direct `GEMINI_API_KEY`, only in secure environment/CI secrets or local `.env.local`, never source or logs. Direct Gemini transcription uploads must be cleaned up and failures reported; OpenRouter audio is sent inline and does not create a matching Gemini uploaded-file cleanup obligation.
- If the approved voice profile, configured transport or real word timestamps are unavailable, diagnose the root cause and report a precise blocker. **Only when the owner has explicitly enabled** `HSK_AI_OPENROUTER_FALLBACK_ENABLED=true` on a Gemini Direct first route may an HTTP 429 or transient HTTP 5xx switch to the approved OpenRouter model/voice transport. This is a separate paid provider and must have an account spending cap and RU/TG A/B voice approval before production activation. Never rotate Google keys on a quota error, silently change models, mix TTS providers after an audio chunk is saved, regenerate an existing good take, invent timestamps, or bypass access controls. STT fallback must process the same saved speech. Prioritize natural RU/TG and correct Mandarin in the first TTS attempt. **Owner decision 2026-11-08** (after listening to the bi-comparison RU/TG A/B): the whole approved narration of one language/package is synthesized as **one take** by default; per-scene takes (`HSK_AI_TTS_MODE=scene`) only on an explicit owner request.
- Treat the voice name in the Gemini request as the requested voice identity; still listen to the generated voice in the rendered sample before approving the profile.

## Visuals, timing and QA

- **Characters and lesson art (owner rules 2026-11-09):** when a frame needs a character, use only the HSK AI cast in `content/characters/hsk-ai-characters.json` (exact app vectors) — never a human and never an invented or redrawn mascot; incidental distant people never show a recognizable face. Backgrounds come from Codex first, then the paid image fallback; see `content/WORKFLOW.md` step 2 and `content/EDITING_SPEC.md#illustrated-glass-lesson-format`.

- For dated HSK lesson videos, keep the existing React/Remotion renderer. Separate premium promotions and advanced standalone motion videos may use HyperFrames according to the canonical engine-selection policy in `content/SKILLS.md`; never silently migrate existing lessons. Apply `content/visual-system.json` for quantitative design values and `content/EDITING_SPEC.md` for transitions, captions, sound and shot composition. The dated lesson format is 9:16 at 1080×1920. The 50-second duration target is a pacing hint only: there is no 45-second minimum or fixed maximum for lessons or ads. Keep the full narration and CTA at their natural duration; do not pad with silence, stretch audio or truncate speech to reach a target. Make the video concise and complete, and let its length follow the material. Preserve approved/published historical snapshots.
- Build meaningful synchronized scenes—not a static slideshow. Every important spoken concept must correspond to an accurately recognizable object/action/change at its real word cue; plan the visual requirement **before** TTS. An unrelated but animated graphic fails QA. Verify before/cue/settled poses at full frame and 270×480, single readable subtitle/pinyin treatment, and correct RU/TG-specific word timings. Mechanical tests alone do not prove visual meaning.
- Use genuine, **current** product screenshots. If the old capture is stale/missing, the production agent should independently open the real HSK AI PWA in an available authorized browser environment, take the needed locale/viewport screenshot and verify source revision, exact crop, visibility and provenance without requesting manual captures from the owner. If browser/auth access is unavailable, record a truthful blocker; never fabricate UI or bypass login. Avoid third-party branded copying.
- Narration edits invalidate imported voice and caption timing. Any content/visual change invalidates the render, final publication preview and prior package approval; regenerate and inspect the updated package.
- Validate video duration, voice/caption match, pinyin, Chinese content, design safety margins, music/effects, encoded video, sidecar captions and metadata before calling a review package complete.
- **Agent root-cause recovery (owner approved 2026-11-05):** During authorized video preparation, ChatGPT/Codex diagnoses any failing script, visual, cue, test or render, fixes the actual cause, reruns the affected checks, and continues. Limit identical targeted visual repair attempts to two; then change from blind retry to root-cause investigation and bounded regression tests. Never claim completion without evidence, overwrite approved/published artifacts, publish without exact-owner-preview authorization, or bypass security and permissions.
