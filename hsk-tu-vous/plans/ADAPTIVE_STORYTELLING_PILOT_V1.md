## Chinese evidence already in the plans (must still check source receipts before rewriting)
- `会` (huì, learned ability) vs `能` (néng, situational ability). `我会游泳。` (Wǒ huì yóuyǒng. — I can swim / I learned how). `我今天不能游泳。` (Wǒ jīntiān bù néng yóuyǒng. — I cannot swim today).
- `有点儿` (yǒudiǎnr, a little / rather) vs `一点儿` (yìdiǎnr, a little). `这个有点儿贵。` (Zhège yǒudiǎnr guì. — A little expensive). `便宜一点儿，可以吗？` (Piányi yìdiǎnr, kěyǐ ma? — Could it be a little cheaper?).
- `还是` (háishi, “or” in a choice question) vs `或者` (huòzhě, “or” in a statement). `你喝茶还是喝咖啡？` (Nǐ hē chá háishi hē kāfēi? — Tea or coffee?). `茶或者咖啡都行。` (Chá huòzhě kāfēi dōu xíng. — Either is fine).

## Pilot safety and measurement
- Initial comparison is **editorial**, not a claim that these topics or creative format variants are matched experiments; videos are not yet proven equivalent.
- Before controlled publication, pre-register each hook version and narrative pattern; preserve the same central CTA, approved voice profile, verified Chinese facts and normal owner preview sign-off. Do not rewrite existing published/approved artifacts.
- For a retention experiment, use confirmed fixed post-age 7d data and verified episode/video/post attribution, separate Instagram from YouTube, and compare matched HSK level and duration bands. The published performance snapshots dated 2026-10-26 have 0 verified episode attribution and only lifetime windows. No controlled 7d baseline is available; therefore no winner or uplift can be calculated.
- At least 3 comparable posts per format with at least 500 views each is a **minimum review gate** from Hook Lab, not confidence or statistical significance. Twelve posts could be a first descriptive pilot, not causal proof.
- Record cost per draft: editorial revisions, Gemini requests, voice regenerations, render attempts, review time and post measurement availability. Do not assign dollar costs without provider usage receipts.
- Abort/revert a proposed re-hook if it duplicates the opening, adds unverified Chinese, changes an approved narration unexpectedly, inflates video length without a new learner payoff, or weakens readability.
